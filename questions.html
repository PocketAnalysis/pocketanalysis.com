Before you choose your report

Competitor and market evidence for UK small businesses, with personal review before delivery.

Which report should I choose?

Choose Snapshot (£49) for a concise 8-page starting point, Deep Dive (£79) for a fuller 12-page assessment, or Monthly Monitor (£130 per calendar month) for four 12-page reports covering changes over time.

What happens after payment?

Your payment receipt and a separate secure questionnaire email explain the next step. Complete the questionnaire for the package you purchased. We research your business, personally review the report and email your PDF to you.

When will I receive my report?

Delivery timing starts when we receive your completed brief: up to 24 hours for Snapshot, 12 hours for Deep Dive and 8 hours for the first Monthly report. Later Monthly reports follow the paid cycle at seven-day intervals.

What if the questionnaire email is missing?

Check Spam or Junk, then use the secure brief-link recovery page for your purchased package or email hello@pocketanalysis.com. Never share your private questionnaire link publicly.

What evidence is included?

We use public sources and dated evidence. Prices are compared on a like-for-like basis where possible. Estimates and assumptions are labelled; unavailable search volumes or other data are not presented as measured facts.

Can I cancel Monthly Monitor?

Yes. Cancellation stops future renewal payments. You retain the remaining reports from your paid four-report cycle; refunds are handled separately.
